# Ember & Olive — Cinematic Restaurant Website

## Vision
Create a premium, editorial Mediterranean restaurant website centered on live-fire cooking. The experience will use charcoal, warm ivory, olive, and copper tones; elegant serif headlines; refined sans-serif body copy; cinematic imagery; and restrained, purposeful motion.

## Pages
- **Home** — cinematic full-screen opening, editorial introduction, signature dishes, story, chef, menu preview, immersive dining scene, horizontal gallery, testimonials, private dining, reservation callout, journal, newsletter, footer.
- **Our Story** — restaurant philosophy, fire-and-olive narrative, provenance, chef profile, timeline.
- **Menu** — interactive categories with polished dish descriptions and realistic pricing.
- **Experience** — dining room, open hearth, bar, hospitality, and private dining.
- **Gallery** — immersive responsive image collection with interactive viewing.
- **Journal** — editorial stories and article previews.
- **Reservations** — elegant booking form and service details.
- **Contact** — location, hours, contact details, and practical visit information.

## Visual & Motion Direction
- Full-bleed restaurant/fire imagery with layered smoke, ember particles, and restrained glass effects.
- Large editorial typography, asymmetric compositions, fine borders, generous spacing, and sharp-to-subtle corner radii.
- Page-load reveals, staggered typography, scroll-triggered image masks, parallax, slow image zoom, hover transforms, magnetic-feeling calls to action, animated underlines, counters, and section transitions.
- Horizontal food gallery and testimonial slider with touch-friendly controls.
- Compact sticky navigation after scrolling and smooth route/page transitions.
- Reduced-motion support and performance-conscious animation behavior.

## Implementation
- Establish reusable semantic design tokens and typography in the global style system.
- Build shared navigation, footer, motion primitives, image treatments, and reusable editorial sections.
- Generate a cohesive set of cinematic restaurant imagery and use it across all pages.
- Add dedicated metadata for every page and ensure responsive behavior from mobile through wide desktop.
- Verify navigation, interactions, visual rendering, and reduced-motion behavior in the running site.

## Content Assumptions
Use polished fictional content for Ember & Olive, including believable Mediterranean dishes, pricing, testimonials, editorial titles, opening hours, address, phone, and email. These practical details can be replaced with the restaurant’s real information later.
